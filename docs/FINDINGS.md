be regenerated by re-running the pipeline. When findings here disagree
with the database, **the database wins**.

## T1 — Smoking gun

### F1. The 1947 FBI file invokes "extraterrestrial" as a working hypothesis

`record.id = 1, 7, 8, 15` — FBI 62-HQ-83894 sections 7, 9, 10, and Serial 449.

Internal FBI text:

> "We believe that most of the objects which have been sighted have been of **extraterrestrial**…"
> *(Section 10)*

> "the possibility that **extraterrestrial animals** were flying into our atmosphere"
> *(Section 7)*

> "Possible communication with **extraterrestrial vehicles** from another planet"
> *(Section 9)*

These phrases appear in the FBI's own analyst-written sections, not in
news clippings or third-party material. The 62-HQ-83894 case file dates
from June 1947 to July 1968 and includes the Maury Island incident.

### F2. State Department cable: "EXTRATERRESTRIAL AND UNDER INTELLIGENT CONTROL"

`record.id = 149` — `dos-uap-d2-cable-2-kazakhstan-january-1994.pdf`.

Formal U.S. diplomatic cable from the embassy in Kazakhstan, January 31, 1994.
The cable summary:

> "…SEEMED TO SUPPORT, THAT THE OBJECT WAS **EXTRATERRESTRIAL** AND UNDER
> **INTELLIGENT CONTROL**."

Single-source primary government document explicitly endorsing an
extraterrestrial-and-intelligent-control hypothesis as a finding worth cabling
to Washington.

### F3. State Department cable: Mexican Congress alien-life testimony (manifest mislabeled as 2003)

`record.id = 152` — `059uap00013.pdf`, manifest title "State Department UAP Cable 5,
Mexico, September 16, 2003".

The cable's own metadata: `MRN: 23 MEXICO 2544; Date/DTG: Sep 16, 2023 / 160150Z SEP 23`.
Subject: *"Mexico: Weekly Political Blotter, Sep 11–15"* — including the bullet:

> "Mexican Congress Hears Testimony on Alien Life"

…describing Maussan's September 12, 2023 presentation of "non-human bodies"
to the Chamber of Deputies. **The manifest CSV records this as 2003 (a year
typo for 2023).**

The cable also documents its own internal release pipeline:

> "Released in Full — John Powers, Acting-Director, US Department of State, 2/25/2026"

So the cable was internally cleared for declassification on **February 25,
2026** — over two months before the May 8, 2026 public disclosure.

### F4. "UFOs and Defense — What Should We Prepare For"

`record.id = 19` — `255_413270_UFO's_and_Defense_What_Should_we_Prepare_For.pdf`.

Strongest single record (6 themes; matches more T1+T2 rules than any
other doc). Section structure includes:

> "Appendix 5 — The Roswell Affair — Disinformation"
> "Roswell: indisputable facts"
> "Army and extraterrestrials baptized **EBEs, Extraterrestrial Biological Entities**"
> "Bennewicz disclosed this information to American **saucerists**"

Bennewicz here is Paul Bennewicz, the Albuquerque engineer documented
as the target of a 1980s AFOSI disinformation campaign (the "Aviary"
affair, Richard Doty, etc.). The doc treats EBE as a defined term and
splits Roswell into "indisputable facts" vs "disinformation" sections —
a primary analytical document, not a news clipping.

## T2 — Strong signal (selected)

### F5. The 1950s "weather balloon / cosmic ray balloon" cover narrative is documented in real time

`record.id = 5, 6, 7` — FBI 62-HQ-83894 sections 5, 6, 7.

> "Liddel is sponsoring the cosmic experiments."
> "large numbers of meteorological and **cosmic ray balloons** for experimental purposes"

Dr. Urner Liddel was a Navy physicist who publicly attributed UFO
sightings to skyhook / cosmic-ray balloons in 1951 — a public-facing
narrative that ran in parallel to the still-classified Project Mogul.
The FBI sections show that narrative being *tracked internally* during
the same period.

### F6. The FBI was monitoring 1950s UFO contactee literature

`record.id = 1, 15` — FBI sections 10 and Serial 449.

> "THE ADVENT OF THE COSMIC VIEWPOINT by Bryant Reeve — $6.15."
> "CALVIN GIRVIN — 'I Was an Agent for the Extraterrestrials' — Ch: Wes Bateman"
> "Frank E.B. Stranges' 4th Annual UFO Space and…"

The FBI logged civilian UFO contactee book titles, prices, and authors
in their case files. Bryant Reeve, Calvin Girvin, Frank E. Stranges
were all real 1950s/60s UFO contactee figures.

### F7. Maury Island incident is in primary source

`record.id = 2, 3, 5, 33` — FBI sections 1, 2, 3, 5.

> "THEIR REPORT OF SEEING SOME FLYING DISCS OVER MAURY ISLAND, WN…"
> "I had seen on Maury Island. DA professed reluctance to tell the story…"
> "THERE IS NO QUESTION OF THE CRASH OF THE B-25 AT KELSO"
> "The bodies were recovered and transported in the ambulance to McCHORD"

The Maury Island case (June 1947) — where two FBI agents (Brown and Davidson)
died in a B-25 crash near Kelso, WA after collecting alleged UFO debris from
Harold Dahl. *"The bodies were recovered"* refers to the FBI agents, **not
aliens** — easy to misread.

### F8. Mission Reports use a formal anomaly classification form

`record.id = 42, 43, 44, 45, 46, 47, 48, 49, 73, 74` — DOW-UAP-D series.

Every Mission Report uses a structured field literally titled:

> "UAP Anomalous Characteristics/Behaviors:"

…followed by free-text descriptions like "MAINTAINED STEADY FLIGHT",
"LIGHT/GLARE FROM…", and the form-template question:

> "UAP Under Intelligent Control (yes/no; if yes, describe…)"

The Pentagon has institutionalized this categorization. CENTCOM,
INDOPACOM, AFRICOM, NORTHCOM, Department of the Army, and Department
of the Air Force all submit reports using the same form.

## T3 — Notable forensic / provenance

### F9. NASA Apollo "anomalous lights" photos were edited in Adobe Illustrator

12 files (6 full-size PNGs + 6 thumbnails): `nasa-uap-vm[1-6]-apollo-*`.

Every NASA Apollo VM image carries `Software: Adobe Illustrator 30.1
(Windows)` in EXIF. The page captions say *"yellow box contains an
enlarged area of the original photo"* — that's a vector overlay added
in Illustrator. Worth verifying whether anything else was modified.

### F10. The NASA PDFs are a 4-step manufacturing chain

`record.id = ...nasa-uap-d4..d7` — Apollo + Skylab debriefing PDFs.

PDF Info dict reveals:

1. **HP 9100C Digital Sender** — 1990s/2000s networked document scanner that
   scanned the original paper.
2. **Mac Preview / macOS 26.4 Quartz `AppendMode 1.1`** — multiple PDFs
   merged on a Mac.
3. **Adobe Acrobat 26 Paper Capture Plug-in** — Adobe's OCR added a text
   layer.
4. **AES-256 soft encryption with `print:yes copy:no`** applied for the
   release.

### F11. The "Western US Event" briefing was made on release morning

`record.id = ?` — `western_us_event_slides_5.08.2026.pdf`.

`pdf_creator: Microsoft® PowerPoint® for Microsoft 365`,
`pdf_created_at: Fri May 8 06:46:59 2026 EDT` — the slide deck was
created in PowerPoint **the morning of the release**. This is likely
the deck that accompanied any press conference or media availability.

### F12. The 2002 outlier

One PDF in the corpus has `pdf_created_at: Mon Mar 11 07:00:48 2002 EST`,
predating the disclosure narrative by 24 years. This is good signal:
the doc *existed for over two decades* before being released. (Earlier
draft of "the gov has been sitting on stuff" thesis.)

### F13. 109 PDFs ship with intentional copy-restrictions

109 of 116 PDFs were shipped with AES-256 encryption and `print:yes
copy:no change:no addNotes:yes`. This is **soft encryption** — designed
to friction text extraction without preventing it (qpdf strips it
trivially). The *intent* is to slow down analysts; the *effect* is
notable on its own.

### F14. Three Mission Reports are corrupted

D63 (Strait of Hormuz, October 2020), D64 (Iran, November 2020), D65
(Persian Gulf, July 2020) — all 2020 Middle East events — fail
`pdftotext` with malformed-dictionary errors. They render in Preview
but can't be parsed by standard tools. Could be sloppy redaction
tooling that left invalid PDF objects, or could be intentional
obfuscation.

| Issue | Detail |

## Methodology + limitations

- All findings derive from automated extraction (`pdftotext`, tesseract,
  exiftool, pdfinfo) plus FTS5 keyword search. False positives caught
  during keyword development are noted in `apps/classifier/README.md`.
- OCR'd text from scanned PDFs has noise; specific phrasings should be
  verified against the source PDF before quoting in external work.
- This document captures findings as of the first run on 2026-05-09.
  Re-running the pipeline may surface additional findings as keyword
  rules evolve.
- Nothing here is a UAP/UFO claim by us. The findings document what
  the gov *itself* wrote, what they *included* in the release, and
  what their *metadata* reveals about how the docs were prepared.
